# 008 — Backdrop: white mushroom cultivation lab

Follow the **Backdrop tasks** rules in `tasks/README.md`.

## The scene
A bright, clean, white mushroom cultivation lab: white walls and floor, stainless-steel shelving racks filled with clear grow bags and jars with mushrooms fruiting out of them (oyster clusters in cream and grey, a few pink oyster, shaggy white lion's mane), a laminar flow hood on a steel bench along one wall, petri dishes and agar plates stacked neatly, a humidifier giving off a soft mist, a small window or glass door on the LEFT. Orderly and scientific, but with the cheerful character of the rest of the site.

## Light
Clean, bright, slightly cool white light from overhead panels, plus soft daylight from the LEFT so the key light still comes from the left. White surfaces with gentle pale-blue and grey shadows. This scene is deliberately cooler than the warm reno room.

## Spot to leave empty
- **Rachel's spot:** open white floor at about 45–68% of the width, lower half, in front of the shelving. Anything she works with (a trolley, a tray, a spray bottle) comes with her drawing later.

## Phone crop
Rachel's spot and at least one rack of fruiting mushrooms should fall inside the 30–75% width band.

## Must have
- Unmistakably a mushroom-growing lab: fruiting mushrooms clearly visible on the racks
- Clean and tidy, mostly white
- 4–6 hiding nooks (under the bottom shelf, behind a jar, beside the humidifier, on a bench corner, by a floor drain)

## Avoid
- Red, yellow or white **toadstools with spots** anywhere (those are the collectible mushrooms; keep the lab's mushrooms to oyster and lion's mane types)
- Labels with readable text on jars or equipment
- Horror or "mad scientist" mood, dirt or mould

## QC pass criteria (Wren checks these)
- Reads instantly as a mushroom cultivation lab
- Style matches `scene/room-empty.png` (same show), even though the palette is cooler
- Key light from the left; Rachel's spot clear; key features in the phone band
- No people, no readable text, no spotted toadstools
