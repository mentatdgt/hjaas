# 012 — Logo: a real metal pin with one big idea (six options)

## Why 011 didn't work (don't repeat these)
- **It looked like plastic.** The gold was a flat, even yellow-tan with soft gradients. Edges were fat and pillowy, the white highlight blobs looked like an inflated sticker, everything was equally glossy, and the plain white background gave the metal nothing to reflect.
- **The designs were generic.** All six were the word "HJaaS" in a stock retro frame (planet, starburst, rocket, shield) with the same lettering. Nothing was specific to her, and no idea made you smile.

## The brand
**HJaaS = "Hui Jie as a Service."** A witty spoof of tech "Software as a Service" naming. Hui Jie (Rachel) is an interior designer who does everything for her friends: carpentry and renovation, interior design, video production, mushroom farming and travel.

The logo should be **punchy, witty and collectible**: one clever idea, a bold silhouette you'd recognise from across a room, in mid-century retro-futurist style (the 1950s–60s space age as imagined then: tin toys, Googie signs, board-game spinners, chrome). It should be grown-up, never cute or childish.

**Original only.** Evoke the era, not any specific film, show or brand: no existing logos, title lettering, characters or ships.

## The look: photograph of a real metal pin (this is the main fix)
Make each option look like a **macro product photograph of a real, premium die-struck pin**, not a 3D render, sticker or illustration:
- **Real metal:**
  - Polished plating has high-contrast reflections. Most of the surface mirrors a dark room, with a few crisp bright bands from the light, so it looks like metal and not paint.
  - Choose the plating that suits each design: polished gold, bright chrome or nickel, or black nickel. Rockets, robots and the service bell suit chrome.
- **Mixed finishes on every pin**, which is what makes premium pins look expensive:
  - Polished raised lines.
  - Some areas of bare sandblasted (matte, fine-grain) metal next to polished metal.
  - At least one area of **translucent enamel over engraved metal** (a sunburst or wave texture glowing through the colour, like jewellery).
  - Optionally, one small area of glitter enamel, e.g. flames or stars.
- **Soft enamel, no epoxy dome:**
  - Colour fills sit slightly recessed below crisp metal walls, with a semi-gloss surface and a tiny meniscus at the edges.
  - No thick clear coating and no white highlight blobs.
- **Crisp, thin edges:** sharp bevelled metal lines, and visible edge thickness of ~1.5 mm with a polished rim. Nothing puffy or rounded like plastic.
- **Photography:**
  - Shot straight on, the pin lying flat on **dark charcoal wool felt** (the fabric texture should be visible).
  - One large soft light from the upper left plus a thin rim light, so the metal has something to reflect.
  - A tiny contact shadow on the felt. Sharp focus across the whole pin.
- **Colour:** punchy and limited. Use the plating plus at most three enamel colours from cream `#FBF3EA`, tomato red `#D9483B`, mustard `#E9B949`, retro teal `#2E8C86`, deep navy `#1F2A44`, ink `#2A1B14`. Strong contrast between neighbouring colours.

## Lettering
- Give each option **its own lettering with personality** that fits the idea: atomic-age script, chunky extended italic with speed lines, chrome inline letters, or engraved small caps. Don't use the same serif everywhere.
- The idea and the silhouette carry the logo. "HJaaS" can be secondary in size where that's stronger.
- **Spelling is exactly** `HJaaS` (capital H, capital J, lowercase a a, capital S), plus `HUI JIE AS A SERVICE` and `SPIN FOR SERVICE` where used. Letters must be crisp and correct.
