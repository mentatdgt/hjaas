# Tasks — instructions for Quinn (ChatGPT)

Each file here is one animation. Read the whole task file **and** every reference image it lists before generating.

## Delivery spec (applies to every task unless the task says otherwise)

1. **One image, all frames.** Put every frame in a single sprite sheet, laid out in the grid the task gives (e.g. 4 columns × 2 rows), read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Do not generate frames as separate images: one image keeps the character identical across frames.
2. **Equal cells, invisible grid.** Every cell is the same size. Do **not** draw grid lines, borders, frame numbers, labels, captions or arrows.
3. **Background.** Transparent PNG. If transparency isn't possible, use a flat pure green `#00FF00` background with no gradient, texture, floor or shadow.
4. **Same size, same spot.** The character is the same height in every cell and stands on the same invisible floor line. Keep at least 8% empty margin on every side of every cell. Nothing may cross into a neighbouring cell.
5. **Only the described motion changes.** Outfit, colours, proportions, line weight, hairstyle and accessories stay exactly the same as the reference sheet in every frame.
6. **Match the reference style**: same line style, colouring and level of detail as the reference sheet. Don't add rendering, lighting or texture the reference doesn't have, **unless the task gives a Lighting section**: then follow that section (scene tasks light Rachel to match the room).
7. **Size.** Make the sheet as large as you can. Phones show Rachel cropped and enlarged, so more pixels per frame means crisper art.
8. If something in the task is impossible or unclear, say so in your reply instead of guessing.

## Backdrop tasks (location paintings, no characters)

Backdrop tasks ignore rules 1–4 above and follow these instead:

1. **One full-bleed painting**, landscape 16:9, as large as you can make it. No transparency, no green, no border.
2. **Match `scene/room-empty.png`**: same painted cartoon style, outline weight, texture, colour richness and level of detail. Every backdrop should look like it belongs to the same show.
3. **Light comes from the LEFT** in every backdrop (the site lights Rachel from the left everywhere). The task says what kind of light.
4. **Empty of people and animals.** No characters, crowds, silhouettes or reflections of people.
5. **No readable text**: no signs, logos, brand names or shop names. Shapes of signage are fine; letters are not.
6. **Leave the marked spots empty.** The task names where Rachel (and anyone else) will be placed later. Keep that floor area clear and unobstructed, with nothing in front of it. Anything she will hold, sit on or touch is drawn with her later, not in the backdrop.
7. **Phone crop.** Portrait phones show only the band from about 30% to 75% of the image width. Rachel's spot and the location's key feature must sit inside that band; the rest of the room is for swiping.
8. **Hiding nooks.** Include 4–6 natural little nooks where a tiny mushroom could hide (under a shelf, beside a pot, on a ledge, in a shadow). Don't draw mushrooms there.
9. Camera at roughly standing eye level with gentle perspective, like the reno room.

## Revisions

If the task has a **Revision notes** section with an open round, fix exactly what it lists, keep everything that passed, and regenerate the **whole** sheet.
