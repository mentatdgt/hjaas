# M01: Rachel's model sheet (the master look)

**Why:** Rachel has drifted towards anime and cosy illustration, especially in the café: eyeliner and lashes, almond eyes, glossy strand-by-strand hair, gradient skin and blush. The target is the original look: a **Gravity Falls-style** 2D cartoon. Draw her in that *style* only, as an original character (never a character from the show).

## The style rules (write these into every later Rachel task)
| Feature | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Eyes | Big round **white circles with small black dot pupils**, thick outline. A simple curved line when closed. | Lashes, eyeliner, almond shapes, irises, sparkles. **No glasses**: no rim, bridge or arms. The round whites *are* the eyes. |
| Brows | Simple thick curved strokes | Detailed, tapered brows |
| Face | Simple rounded shape, small nose as a single curve, simple mouth line | Blush gradients, lip colour, detailed noses |
| Hair | Dark brown, loose and wavy, drawn as **a few big solid shapes**: one base tone plus one shadow tone, a few interior lines | Many strands, glossy highlights, gradients |
| Shading | **Flat colour plus one hard-edged shadow tone** | Soft gradients, airbrush, glow, rim light on skin |
| Lines | Thick, even, dark outlines | Thin, varying, painterly lines |
| Proportions | Slightly stylised: larger head, simple hands with rounded fingers | Realistic proportions, detailed hands |
| Kept | Warm tan skin, gold round pendant on a thin cord, outfit 1 (black tank, olive cargo pants, brown boots) | — |

## Deliver: `M01_rachel_model.png` (one sheet, plain light background, no text)
1. Full body, front view, standing relaxed.
2. Full body, three-quarter view.
3. Four head close-ups: neutral; warm smile looking at the viewer; eyes closed and content; looking down at work.
4. Two hand studies: holding a stylus; holding a cup.

**Stop for the user's approval** before using it anywhere.
