# 017: Rachel in the lab, key poses

## The scene
Rachel stands at a small stainless lab trolley in the white mushroom lab, tending a tray of oyster mushrooms. Her idle routine alternates slowly and calmly, never hurried:
- **misting** the tray with a small spray bottle, a few unhurried sprays;
- then **writing notes on a clipboard**, with a glance at the tray;
- then back to misting, and so on.

When a visitor finds a mushroom, she looks up at them, beams and gives a thumbs-up (same idea as scene 1).

## Make every pose like this
- **One image per pose.** Same canvas size, same framing, same scale and same position of her feet and of the trolley in every image, so the poses line up when swapped.
- **Flat pure green background `#00FF00`**, or transparent. No floor, no shadow, no room.
- **Full body, standing,** in three-quarter view, turned slightly toward the viewer's LEFT, where the trolley is. She stands behind and to the right of the trolley.
- **The trolley and tray are part of every image:** a small two-shelf stainless trolley at waist height, with a shallow tray of pale oyster mushrooms on top. The clipboard (pale wood, white paper) lies on the trolley beside the tray whenever she isn't holding it.
- **Her look matches `reference/hjaas6.png` exactly:** lab coat, dark top, gold pendant, olive cargo pants, brown boots, round glasses, low ponytail with a dark tie, warm skin tone. No bandana.
- **Style matches the approved scene-1 Rachel:** the same line weight, flat colours and soft cel shading. Key light from the LEFT, slightly cool, to suit the white lab.
- **Make it as large as possible.** She should be at least 900 px tall in the image.

## The eight poses
| # | Name | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `A1_mist_aim` | **Misting, at rest:** spray bottle in her right hand, aimed down at the tray, finger on the trigger. Left hand resting on the trolley's edge. Looking at the tray, calm and focused, soft smile. **This is the base pose:** poses 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8 must match it everywhere except where described. |
| 2 | `A2_mist_squeeze` | Same as 1, but the trigger is squeezed and a soft, fine mist cone sprays over the tray (light, translucent, small). Nothing else moves. |
| 3 | `A3_mist_lift` | Same as 1, but the bottle is lifted about 10 cm and tilted, as between sprays. She glances at the mist settling. |
| 4 | `B1_write` | **Writing:** the bottle is set down on the trolley. She holds the clipboard in her left hand at chest height, writing with a pen in her right hand. Looking down at the clipboard. Same body position and feet as pose 1. |
| 5 | `B2_glance` | Same as 4, but her eyes and head turn slightly toward the tray, checking something; the pen pauses. |
| 6 | `A1_blink_half` | Exactly pose 1, eyes half closed behind the glasses. |
| 7 | `K1_beam` | From pose 1: she lifts her head and looks **straight at the viewer** with a big, warm, beaming smile (like expression 03 "Delighted" in `hjaas7.png`). The bottle is still in her hand, lowered a little; the left hand stays on the trolley. |
| 8 | `K2_thumbs` | Same as 7, but her **left** hand comes up off the trolley in a confident thumbs-up beside her shoulder. Same beaming face. |

## Avoid
- Changing her outfit, hair, glasses or proportions between poses.
- Moving her feet or the trolley between poses.
- Drawing a room, a floor or shadows.
- A hurried or exaggerated look. This scene is calm and careful.
- Text or labels inside the images.
