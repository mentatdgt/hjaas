# 011 — Logo: retro soft-enamel pin, six options

## The brand
**HJaaS = "Hui Jie as a Service."** A witty spoof of tech "Software as a Service" naming, for a real interior designer who takes on odd jobs for friends: carpentry and renovation, interior design, video production, mushroom farming, travel. The logo should feel **grown-up, crafted and collectible**: something you'd proudly pin on a denim jacket. Not cute, not childish.

## The look: a real soft-enamel pin
Render every option as a **photographic-quality soft enamel pin**, straight-on front view:
- **Raised polished metal lines** (gold plating) separating the colour areas, catching the light
- **Enamel fills slightly recessed** below the metal lines, with a **glossy finish** and soft specular highlights
- A **clear epoxy-dome sheen** across the surface, with one or two crisp highlight streaks
- **Die-cut outline** following the design's shape, with a thin metal edge all round
- Lighting: soft studio light from the **upper left** (matching the site), no harsh reflections, no drop shadow, no pin back, no backing card, no hand

## Era and mood
**Mid-century retro-futurism:** 1950s–60s space-age optimism, Googie diner signs, atomic starbursts, ringed planets, rocket fins, art-deco sunbursts, vintage tin toys and board-game medallions, and chrome-edged display lettering. Think "the future, as imagined in 1958."

**Original only.** Evoke the era, not any specific film, TV show or brand: no existing logos, title lettering, characters, ships or symbols from any franchise.

## Rules for every option
- **Spelling exactly** `HJaaS` (capital H, capital J, lowercase a a, capital S) and `HUI JIE AS A SERVICE` where used. Letters must be crisp and correctly spelled.
- **Palette:** gold-plated metal lines, plus at most four enamel colours from: cream `#FBF3EA`, tomato red `#D9483B`, mustard `#E9B949`, retro teal `#2E8C86`, deep navy `#1F2A44`, ink brown `#2A1B14`.
- **Readable small:** in the bottom-right of each cell, add a ~64 px preview of the same pin. It must still read as the logo.
- **Consistent rendering** across all six (same lighting, same metal, same gloss), so we compare designs, not renders.

## Delivery
One image, 3 columns × 2 rows, plain white background, as large as you can make it. A small number 1–6 in the top-left corner of each cell; no other labels.
