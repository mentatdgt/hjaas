# 009 — Backdrop: mountain lookout (travel)

Follow the **Backdrop tasks** rules in `tasks/README.md`.

## The scene
A rocky mountain lookout at the end of a hiking trail: a flat stone ledge in the foreground with a wooden trail marker post (blank, no writing) and a few tufts of grass and wildflowers, a sweeping view of a green valley with a winding river, layered blue-green mountain ridges into the distance, a few clouds drifting below the far peaks.

## Light
Warm golden late-afternoon light from the LEFT, long soft shadows to the right, hazy warm glow on the far ridges. Matches the warmth of the reno room.

## Spot to leave empty
- **Rachel's spot:** flat open rock at about 50–70% of the width, lower half, near the ledge edge (she will stand there in hiking gear, taking in the view). Her backpack and trekking poles come with her drawing later.

## Phone crop
Rachel's spot and the best part of the valley view should fall inside the 30–75% width band.

## Must have
- A real sense of height and distance (strong depth from foreground rock to far ridges)
- 4–6 hiding nooks (rock cracks, grass tufts, behind the trail post, under a ledge lip, beside a small boulder)

## Avoid
- Text on the trail marker or anywhere
- People, hikers, animals, buildings

## QC pass criteria (Wren checks these)
- Reads as a breathtaking trail lookout at a glance
- Style matches `scene/room-empty.png`; light from the left
- Rachel's spot clear; key features in the phone band
- No people, no readable text
