# 010 — Logo: six options to choose from

## The brand
**HJaaS = "Hui Jie as a Service."** A playful spoof of tech "Software as a Service" naming, for a real interior designer who happily takes on odd jobs for friends: carpentry and renovation, interior design, video production, mushroom farming, travel. Tone: warm, witty, handmade, a little cheeky. The site is drawn in a cosy Saturday-morning cartoon style with thick dark outlines, flat colour and warm late-afternoon light.

## Six directions (one per cell, in this order)
| # | Direction |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Hand-lettered wordmark.** Chunky, bouncy "HJaaS" with the two lowercase "aa" in the accent colour, slightly tilted; tiny tool doodles allowed. |
| 2 | **Monogram badge.** "HJ" inside a round badge whose top is shaped like her high messy bun with the grey zig-zag bandana. |
| 3 | **Mascot + wordmark.** Rachel's head (bun, bandana, round glasses, big grin) peeking over the top of the "HJaaS" letters. |
| 4 | **Service stamp.** A round vintage rubber stamp: "HJaaS" in the middle, "HUI JIE AS A SERVICE" around the edge, tiny icons for hammer, pencil, camera, mushroom and plane. |
| 5 | **Startup parody.** A clean rounded app-icon square, like a slick tech logo, but the icon is her bun-and-bandana silhouette; "HJaaS" set beside it in a friendly rounded font. |
| 6 | **Zig-zag mark.** A simple, bold symbol built from the bandana's zig-zag pattern forming an "H" and "J", with "HJaaS" underneath. |

## Rules for every option
- **Original.** No characters, logos, lettering styles or symbols from any TV show, film or brand.
- **Readable tiny.** Must still read as a favicon at 32×32 px. In the bottom-right corner of each cell, add a small preview of the option at about 64 px.
- **Palette.** At most three colours plus the dark outline: warm cream `#FBF3EA`, ink brown `#2A1B14`, tomato red `#D9483B`, mustard `#E9B949`, bandana grey `#6E6878`. Flat colour, no gradients, no photo textures.
- **Spelling exactly** `HJaaS` (capital H, capital J, lowercase a a, capital S) and, where used, `Hui Jie as a Service`.

## Delivery (overrides delivery-spec rules 2–4 for this task)
One image, 3 columns × 2 rows, plain white background. Put a **small number 1–6** in the top-left corner of each cell so we can discuss them; no other labels. Make it as large as you can.

## Self-check loop (max 3 attempts)
Checklist: six distinct directions in the right order · spelling exact in every cell · readable at the 64 px preview · palette respected · original (nothing borrowed) · consistent finish across all six. Mark each ✅/❌/⚠️, regenerate the whole sheet with fixes if any ❌ and attempts remain, deliver the best attempt with an honest report.

## After we pick one
A follow-up task will ask for the chosen logo cleaned up on transparent background, plus an SVG version if you can export one.
