# 006 — Backdrop: Merlion and Marina Bay Sands (video production)

Follow the **Backdrop tasks** rules in `tasks/README.md`.

## The scene
Merlion Park waterfront promenade in Singapore, seen from the classic angle: the white Merlion statue side-on, spouting its arc of water into the bay, and Marina Bay Sands (three towers joined by the long rooftop park) across the water behind it. Calm bay water, a stone promenade with a low railing in the foreground half, a few palm or rain trees at the edges.

## Light
Warm late-afternoon golden light from the LEFT, lighting the Merlion and the Marina Bay Sands towers in warm gold. Soft warm sky, a few clouds. Same overall warmth as the reno room.

## Spots to leave empty (people are added later)
- **Subject spot:** open promenade floor at about 35–50% of the width, lower half, in front of the Merlion (a friend will pose here, with the Merlion behind him).
- **Rachel's spot:** open promenade floor at about 55–72% of the width, lower half (she will stand side-on, filming toward the subject).
Both spots on flat paving, nothing in front of them.

## Phone crop
The Merlion, a clear view of Marina Bay Sands, and both spots should fall inside the 30–75% width band as much as possible. If the Merlion can't fit fully, keep at least its head and water spout inside.

## Must have
- Recognisable Merlion and Marina Bay Sands silhouettes, correct relative scale and placement
- An empty, clean promenade (no tourists, no vendors, no boats with people)
- 4–6 hiding nooks (planter edges, railing posts, bench legs, steps, lamp-post bases)

## Avoid
- Text, logos, flags with writing, ad screens with words
- Nighttime or a light show (that's a different mood)
- Distorted landmark shapes (e.g. MBS with the wrong number of towers)

## QC pass criteria (Wren checks these)
- Reads instantly as Merlion Park with Marina Bay Sands behind
- Style, outline weight and colour richness match `scene/room-empty.png`
- Light from the left, warm golden hour
- Both spots clear; key features inside the phone band
- No people, no readable text
