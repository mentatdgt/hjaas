# 014 — Logo: Tape Measure pin, six variations

## The pick
From 013, the user chose **#6 Tape Measure**: a chrome tape-measure reel with a mustard enamel tape pulled out, "HJaaS" between the tick marks.

Now make **six variations of that idea**. Each one keeps "chrome reel + mustard tape + HJaaS on the tape" recognisable, but tries a different twist.

**What to fix from 013 #6:**
- It was shown at a 3/4 angle like a real object. It must be a **flat pin, straight on**.
- On its own it only says "builder". Some variations should quietly nod to her other services: design, film, mushrooms, travel.

## The brand (reminder)
**HJaaS = "Hui Jie as a Service."** A witty spoof of "Software as a Service". Hui Jie is an interior designer who does everything for her friends: carpentry and renovation, interior design, video production, mushroom farming and travel. Mid-century retro-futurist mood; grown-up, punchy and collectible. No people or faces. Original only: no real tool brand's design, logo or lettering.

## The look: keep 013's metal, but make it a real flat pin
Make each option look like a **macro product photograph of a real, premium die-struck pin**, not a 3D render, sticker or illustration:
- **Real metal:**
  - Polished plating has high-contrast reflections. Most of the surface mirrors a dark room, with a few crisp bright bands from the light, so it looks like metal and not paint.
  - Choose the plating that suits each design: polished gold, bright chrome or nickel, or black nickel. Domes, globes and reels suit chrome.
- **Mixed finishes on every pin**, which is what makes premium pins look expensive:
  - Polished raised lines.
  - Some areas of bare sandblasted (matte, fine-grain) metal next to polished metal.
  - At least one area of **translucent enamel over engraved metal** (a sunburst or wave texture glowing through the colour, like jewellery).
  - Optionally, one small area of glitter enamel, e.g. flames or stars.
- **Soft enamel, no epoxy dome:**
  - Colour fills sit slightly recessed below crisp metal walls, with a semi-gloss surface and a tiny meniscus at the edges.
  - No thick clear coating and no white highlight blobs.
- **Crisp, thin edges:** sharp bevelled metal lines, and visible edge thickness of ~1.5 mm with a polished rim. Nothing puffy or rounded like plastic.
- **Straight on and flat (the main fix from 013):**
  - It's a real lapel pin: a low relief about 2 mm deep, seen **dead straight on** with no perspective or 3/4 angle.
  - Die-cut to the outline. It shouldn't read as a miniature 3D object.
  - The reel is a flat round face with a raised polished rim, and the tape is a flat enamel strip.
- **Photography: white studio, with metal that still looks like metal:**
  - Shot straight on, the pin lying flat on a **clean white seamless background**, so we see how the logo reads on a light page.
  - Light it the way product photographers shoot chrome on white: one large soft light from the upper left, plus **black cards just out of frame**. The black cards give the polished metal crisp dark reflection bands, so it never goes flat yellow-grey or plastic.
  - A tiny, soft contact shadow under the pin. Sharp focus across the whole pin.
- **Colour:** punchy and limited. Use the plating plus at most three enamel colours from cream `#FBF3EA`, tomato red `#D9483B`, mustard `#E9B949`, retro teal `#2E8C86`, deep navy `#1F2A44`, ink `#2A1B14`. Strong contrast between neighbouring colours.

## Colour
Chrome or polished nickel for the reel. **Mustard `#E9B949`** enamel for the tape, with ink `#2A1B14` ticks and lettering. One accent at most: tomato `#D9483B`, used only for the toadstool (4 and 5), or retro teal `#2E8C86`. Keep it restrained and confident.

## Lettering
- "HJaaS" in a crisp, characterful face that suits a measuring tape: condensed 1950s industrial sans, or stencil-like engraved caps with the lowercase "aa" kept.
- Use the same lettering across all six, so we compare layouts, not fonts.
- **Spelling is exactly** `HJaaS` (capital H, capital J, lowercase a a, capital S). Ticks and letters must be crisp and evenly spaced. No random numbers on the tape.
