  - **No coloured enamel and no sunburst on the reel face.**
- **The tape:**
  - **Mustard translucent enamel over an engraved wavy texture.**
  - Dark ink tick marks along both edges, and thin polished chrome edges.
  - A chrome end hook with a rivet at the tip.
  - It comes out of the lower right of the reel in a gentle S-wave.
- **The lettering:** "HJaaS" in the anchor's exact letterforms: a dark ink, slightly rounded geometric sans, wide letters, sitting between the tick marks. Same weight, same style, same spelling (`HJaaS`: capital H, capital J, lowercase a a, capital S).
- **The view and photography:**
  - The anchor's own viewing angle: the reel almost face-on, turned very slightly.
  - Clean white background, soft light from the upper left, black cards giving the dark reflections, a tiny soft contact shadow.
- **The colours:** chrome, sandblasted silver, mustard tape and ink only. Tomato red `#D9483B` is allowed only where a variation adds a toadstool.
