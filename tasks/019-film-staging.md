# 019: Film scene, five staging options (Merlion at night)

Draw **five different options** of the same scene. Each one is a single finished painting with the characters in it. The user picks one, and every later image is an edit of the picked option. So get the people, the car and the landmarks right; the small details can be fixed later.

## The scene (same in all five options)
A night video shoot on the Merlion Park promenade in Singapore. The camera looks back across the bay: Marina Bay Sands behind on the left and the Merlion statue on the right of centre.

- **Night, but a bright and glowing night**, not dark. Deep navy sky. Marina Bay Sands is lit up: warm golden windows in the three towers, a cool blue glow along the long rooftop SkyPark and along the low podium building. The ArtScience Museum (white lotus shape) glows at the far left edge. Lights reflect in the bay water on the left.
- **The Merlion** stands tall, right of centre, rising from behind the car up near the top of the frame. It's floodlit warm white and spouts its arc of water to the LEFT, down into the bay. Draw it the same way as in `scene/merlion.png`.
- **The car:** a sleek silver electric fastback saloon, parked side-on across the middle of the frame on the promenade paving, its rear on the left and its nose on the right. It has a black roof and glass, black lower trim, a thin red light-bar tail light, black multi-spoke wheels with red brake callipers, and flush door handles. **No badges, logos or number-plate text.** Polished paint picks up the city lights.
- **The presenter** (an original character, described only in words): a friendly young East Asian man with short black hair and thin glasses. He wears a black short-sleeved button-up shirt, a small clip-on mic on his collar, a smartwatch, light grey jeans and white sneakers. He stands in front of the car's middle, facing the camera, talking, with both hands up in a lively mid-sentence gesture. He's a supporting character, so give him the same flat cartoon style as Rachel.
- **On the right:** the promenade continues, with a low lit kiosk building, a hedge planter around the Merlion's base, steps with warm strip lights, and a tree at the far right edge. **No other people.**
- **Rachel** is in the **foreground**, seen **from behind**, over her shoulder. She's filming the presenter with a compact mirrorless camera held up at chest height in both hands. Her flip-out screen faces her (we see its back). Her **bandana and messy bun** face us, and loose curly strands frame her face. She wears a black tank top with the gold pendant cord just visible at the back of her neck. She's **cropped just below the waist** by the bottom of the frame. She's the biggest figure in the scene. Turn her head a touch so her ear and cheek show; later she'll look back over her shoulder at the viewer and smile. **No glasses.**

## Light
- The **key light comes from the LEFT**: a warm golden glow from a lamp post just out of frame on the left. It rims Rachel's left shoulder, bun and arm, and the presenter's left side.
- Fill comes from the city: a cool blue from Marina Bay Sands and warm white bounce from the floodlit Merlion.
- Everyone is clearly readable. Faces and the car are well lit, not silhouetted.

## Composition rules (all options)
- 1672×941, landscape. A full-bleed painting: no border, no text, no UI.
- **Phone band:** portrait phones show only about **25–75% of the width**. The presenter's face and hands, the Merlion's head, and Rachel's head and camera must sit inside that band.
- Keep the **four corners fairly calm** (sky, water, paving): the site feathers the edge of the painting.
- Leave **6–8 hiding nooks** for tiny mushrooms: the car's wheel arches, the kerb of the hedge planter, a railing post, the steps, a lamp-post base, a crack in the paving, behind a bollard. Don't draw mushrooms.
- Style: flat colours, thick dark outlines, large heads and small hands, painted backdrop texture like `scene/room-empty.png`.

## Avoid
- Any text, logos, brand names, channel names, location pins, watermarks or video-player UI.
- Glasses on Rachel.
- A dark, murky night, or a light show or lasers.
- Distorted landmarks: Marina Bay Sands has exactly three towers joined by one SkyPark.
- Extra people.
