# 007 — Backdrop: trendy cafe (interior design)

Follow the **Backdrop tasks** rules in `tasks/README.md`.

## The scene
A stylish, design-led cafe interior of the kind an interior designer would love: tall windows on the LEFT, terrazzo or warm timber floor, curved timber counter with a coffee machine in the back, rattan and bentwood chairs, a few round marble tables, plenty of potted plants, pendant lights, an arched shelf niche with ceramics. Tasteful and calm, slightly Singapore shophouse in feel (high ceiling, fan, patterned floor tile accent).

## Light
Warm mid-afternoon daylight streaming in from the windows on the LEFT, soft window-light patterns on the floor and far wall. Cosy, not orange-heavy: a touch more neutral than the reno room, still warm.

## Spot to leave empty
- **Rachel's spot:** a clear patch of floor by the window light at about 45–68% of the width, lower half. She will be added later sitting at her own small table with her iPad (her table and chair come with her drawing), so leave this area as open floor, not furniture.

## Phone crop
Rachel's spot, a window, and the counter should fall inside the 30–75% width band.

## Must have
- Clearly a cafe (counter, coffee machine, cups on shelves) and clearly "designed" (cohesive palette, materials, plants)
- Other tables are empty: no cups left out, no laptops, no people
- 4–6 hiding nooks (plant pots, under the counter overhang, shelf niche, window ledge, chair legs)

## Avoid
- Menu boards or signs with readable words, brand logos on the coffee machine or cups
- Clutter that competes with Rachel's spot

## QC pass criteria (Wren checks these)
- Reads as a trendy, designed cafe at a glance
- Style matches `scene/room-empty.png`; light from the left
- Rachel's spot clear and well lit; key features in the phone band
- No people, no readable text
