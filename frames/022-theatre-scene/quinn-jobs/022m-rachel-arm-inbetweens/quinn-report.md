# Rachel’s arm in-betweens

All 11 frames are 1024 × 1024 PNGs. Each built-in ImageGen prompt used A1 as the edit target and the approved poses as motion references. The prompts called for a curved elbow-led path, A1’s linework and skin tone, the same wooden brush with blue bristles, and placement at the fraction listed below. The positions are visual in-betweens. I composited the generated arm and brush onto A1 to restore the original pixels outside the edit, using the approved A2 hair where lowering the arm exposed a small area behind the shoulder.
