### C1 · high · An asset failure can strand the page

`site/src/engine/scene.ts:444`, `site/src/engine/site.ts:378`, `:460`, `:559`, `:622`. The opening awaits the room, mushroom sprites, full opening cast and logo before presenting usable artwork. A failed sprite, cast frame or logo rejects the opening task and leaves the page in its loading state. A failed scene load is also cached in `loading`, and a rejected switch leaves `busy` set, so later tab clicks cannot recover. The 1.5 s wait caps rehearsal after these downloads. **Fix.** Put a deadline and retry state around required assets, evict rejected scene promises, and release navigation in `finally`. Fall back to the room with playable mushrooms when optional artwork fails. Keep the prior scene and a retry affordance available while a requested room is unavailable.

### C2 · medium · Tabs can undo each other’s saved finds

`site/src/engine/state.ts:37`, `:66`; `site/src/engine/site.ts:149`, `:169`, `:267`, `:387`. Each tab reads progress once and writes its entire snapshot on a find, first view or pan. A second tab opened earlier can erase newer finds or revoke a saved 50/50 simply by panning. A pan saved on a 300 ms timeout can also be lost on immediate reload or close. **Fix.** Store finds with a transactional union or append-only per-spot keys, reconcile storage changes, and write per-scene pan separately with a final `pagehide` flush. Preserve the existing migration and rederive unlock from merged finds.

### C3 · medium · A returning-scene brush can collect mushrooms through the wrong picture

`site/src/engine/site.ts:519`, `site/src/engine/transition.ts:109`, `:193`, `:267`, `:293`; `site/src/engine/site.css:250`, `:340`. On return switches the destination's buttons are active as soon as its plate is mounted, underneath a pointer-transparent copy of the old room. A tap can collect a mushroom whose drawing is hidden, and keyboard focus can land on it. The blank return-visit reveal has the same exposure during its fade. Portrait panning can also move the incoming plate after `brushOver()` sampled its position, misaligning the strokes. **Fix.** Gate collection and focus until the overlay is clear or Skip completes. If panning or a viewport resize invalidates the snapshot, finish that short transition on the current crop. First-visit `prepare()` already sets `inert` and provides a starting point.

### C4 · medium · Landscape can overwrite the remembered portrait crop

`site/src/engine/pan.ts:67`, `:77`, `:126`. When the viewport expands to show the full plate, the browser can clamp `scrollLeft` to zero and dispatch a scroll event. `layout()` returns as soon as panning is inactive, while `onScroll()` accepts the clamped position as a visitor pan and stores roughly the plate midpoint. Rotating back can lose the spot someone was examining. **Fix.** Ignore scroll events while the viewport has no horizontal overflow. Keep the previous portrait centre across layout changes and cover portrait → landscape → portrait after panning toward an edge.

### C5 · medium · Runtime reduced motion and tab visibility leave motion in flight

`site/src/engine/site.ts:320`, `:615`; `site/src/engine/player.ts:118`, `site/src/engine/mushrooms.ts:211`, `site/src/engine/pan.ts:92`, `site/src/engine/logo.ts:69`. A preference change updates controls and the next idle schedule. The active paint, flight, glint, pan drift or character reaction keeps its original animation. The tab visibility handler only stops the scene players, leaving the other motion clocks to browser throttling. A visitor who enables reduced motion mid-intro can see the existing brush continue, and the control and animation state may disagree. **Fix.** Give running motion explicit owners. On a preference change, settle to the same final state with opacity or an instant update, counting any in-flight find only once. On hide, pause transient motion or finish it deliberately and resume from a consistent state.

### C6 · medium · Scene and mount teardown leave assets and a listener behind

`site/src/engine/form.ts:138`, `site/src/engine/site.ts:692`, `site/src/engine/scene.ts:145`, `:638`, `:789`; `site/src/engine/reaction.ts:39`, `site/src/engine/assets.ts:28`, `:108`. `ContactForm` registers a window resize listener that retains the detached form and trigger. `Scene.destroy()` stops players and removes the plate without releasing its asset resources. On ordinary Build navigation, reaction loading can also add decoded frames after `rest()` has finished, since its URLs are registered only after the whole reaction loads. The worker, its object URL and the URL caches have document-wide ownership with no explicit teardown. **Fix.** Add disposal for the form, presentations and scene assets, then use a generation token or cancellation signal for late decodes. Close late bitmaps, release the worker URL and keep shared caches bounded with explicit ownership.

### C7 · medium · Background asset work competes with the phone hunt

`site/src/engine/site.ts:581`, `:679`; `site/src/engine/assets.ts:203`; `site/src/engine/scene.ts:251`, `:318`; `site/qa/counter/trace.json`. After opening, the code downloads and decodes every other room at high fetch priority, about 4.65 MiB of compressed room images even for someone who stays in Build. Tab focus or hover also starts cast downloads, and the clip loader can continue after leaving a scene. Separately, round 2’s 4× CPU traces measure up to seven dropped frames at the tap and a 102 ms task at the fiftieth. Those traces establish the stall, while its precise attribution needs a tap breakdown. **Fix.** Warm at most the likely next room at low priority, cancel or park inactive clip queues, and schedule speculative work on idle. In the tap trace, separate immediate visual acknowledgement from synchronous storage, focus-driven layout and theatre progress work before moving expensive work out of the input frame.

### C8 · low · A damaged fragment crashes routing before the first room

`site/src/engine/site.ts:96`, `:151`, `:572`; `site/index.html:25`. Decoding a malformed percent escape throws before the unknown-scene fallback. The preload also uses a separate raw-fragment map, so valid encoded links can preload a different room than the one the router resolves. **Fix.** Use one guarded fragment normalization and an own-key allowlist in both paths, with Build as the fallback. Test empty, unknown, malformed and percent-encoded names.

### C9 · medium · The release checks miss the failure and persistence paths

`site/package.json:7`, `site/qa/tools/counter.mjs:491`, `site/qa/tools/prod-form.mjs:11`, `site/qa/tools/review01.mjs:40`. Unit tests cover the unlock math and scene geometry. Browser QA splits the flow across a dev-mode hunt with programmatic panning, production traces of the fiftieth find and a production form check that starts at 50. The production QA-flag comparison reports results without assertions. This leaves the earned unlock across a reload, competing tab writes, a missing critical asset and orientation during a brush without release coverage. **Fix.** Add an assertion-based production smoke test for the existing flag comparison and 49 → 50 → reload → form. Add targeted asset failure and retry, concurrent save, a synthesized portrait swipe, and orientation or a live motion-preference change during a brush.

### C10 · low · Filtered asset builds can pair new manifests with old sprites

`site/scripts/build-assets.mjs:158`, `:360`, `:379`; `site/src/engine/scene.ts:226`, `site/src/engine/walk.ts:130`, `:295`. `npm run assets -- pencil` filters the WebP jobs, then still rewrites all cast, lab and reaction manifests. After a delivery changes a frame’s geometry or filename, that partial run can publish unbuilt references or mismatched sprite rectangles. The theatre track is also loaded with an unchecked cast, so a missing named track can throw on the first walk and interrupt the lead player’s frame loop. **Fix.** Scope manifest writes to the selected asset group, stage a full group before replacing the old one, and validate manifest references and track names, frame lists and rates. Keep the lead animation running if an optional walk track fails validation.

### S1 · high · The production prize cannot send, and the planned POST has no abuse controls in this branch

`site/src/engine/submit.ts:18`, `:33`; `site/src/engine/form.ts:15`, `:102`, `:221`. Unlocking currently reveals a notice and an unavailable Send button. Configuring `VITE_FORM_ENDPOINT` would expose the destination URL in the bundle and POST name, email, needs and message as JSON. Browser validation checks presence and email shape, with no length caps. This branch has no receiving handler, server validation, rate limit or spam control. Delivery and retention depend on the service eventually chosen. **Fix.** Treat verified delivery as a launch gate. Put a same-origin handler or equivalent protected provider in front of the mailbox, enforce the field schema, length and size limits, throttle abuse, and keep mail credentials and recipient routing server-side. Test delivery, invalid input, rejection and timeout. Avoid logging message bodies.

### S2 · medium · The 50-find rule is a client-side game gate

`site/src/engine/state.ts:37`, `:82`; `site/src/engine/site.ts:154`, `:173`; `site/src/engine/submit.ts:37`. The form gates on the locally saved set of valid spot IDs, and the IDs and coordinates ship to the visitor. An editable client can claim finds directly and the proposed POST has no completion check. The old saved `unlocked` Boolean is correctly rederived. **Fix.** For a strictly enforced prerequisite, use server-owned per-session progress and a short-lived completion receipt checked on submission. Treat scripted finds as possible since the browser displays the mushrooms. If the owner keeps the static hunt as a gift mechanic, make that trust boundary explicit and protect the endpoint independently as in S1.

### S3 · medium · Security headers are undefined at the deployment boundary

`site/index.html:23`, `:35`; `site/vite.config.ts:4`; `site/src/engine/assets.ts:108`. No checked-in hosting configuration supplies CSP, clickjacking protection, MIME-sniffing protection or a referrer policy. The inline preload and Blob worker need explicit treatment before a strict CSP can ship. **Fix.** Configure and verify response headers on the chosen host. Move the preload stub and worker to same-origin files or permit the precise required sources. Limit `connect-src` to the selected form service, set `frame-ancestors` to the intended embedding policy, add `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and an explicit `Referrer-Policy`, and verify HTTPS and the final policy in a deployed response.

### S4 · medium · Owner-side development tools have broad defaults

`site/vite.config.ts:6`, `site/README.md:222`, `.mcp.json:3`. Vite binds dev and preview to all interfaces by default. A reachable dev instance exposes development code and hunt presets. The project's two MCP launchers also execute npm `@latest` packages, which makes the installed code change without a project change. **Fix.** Bind to loopback by default and opt into LAN only for a phone session. Pin the reviewed MCP package versions and deploy only `site/dist/`.

### S5 · medium · Quinn can attach another job’s images to this job

`tools/quinn/quinn_watch.sh:18`, `:22`, `:29`. The watcher copies every image newer than `RUNNING` from the account-wide generated-image directory. A concurrent Codex session can leave an unrelated image in the current job’s output, and same-named files can overwrite each other. The watcher also redirects logs and reports to pre-existing job paths without checking that those destinations stay under the queue. **Fix.** Capture an output manifest from the run or use a per-job generation directory. Copy only that run’s images, verify job and output paths remain in the queue, and restrict the queue to trusted writers because each job becomes an unattended agent instruction.

### S6 · low · The preview crawl policy is easy to ship by accident

`site/public/robots.txt:1`, `site/index.html:6`. `Disallow: /` blocks crawler access and is copied unchanged into `dist/`. A public preview URL can still be listed based on external links, while a public launch that keeps this file asks crawlers to skip the site. **Fix.** Make the intended discovery policy part of deployment. Use access control for a private preview, or a crawlable `noindex` response for a public preview that should stay out of search. Publish deliberate robots rules at launch. [Google Search Central on `noindex`](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing).
