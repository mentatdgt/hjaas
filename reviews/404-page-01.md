## Checks
1. **Build: pass.** `npm run build` passes and `dist/404.html` exists. `dist/assets/404/` has **10** files, not 9: the brief's 9 package files plus `instrument-sans.woff2`. All 9 match the package byte for byte. `npm test`: 387 of 387 (run before the two edits, which touch only this static page).
2. **Missing path: pass, with one note.** `/this/does/not/exist` from a small static server sending status 404, in Chrome and WebKit, at 1280x800 and iPhone 13. No failed requests, font loaded, no page errors, no sideways scroll. The only console line is the browser's own "Failed to load resource: 404" for the address itself, which any real 404 produces; "zero console errors" can't literally hold there. (`vite preview` was not used: it answers missing paths with `index.html`.)
3. **8 s recording at 1280x800 (Chrome): pass.** 223 video frames, each matched by pixels against the 5 single poses, no arm, and all 10 two-arm pairs: every frame is one single pose. No blank frames. 2 blinks of 3 frames each. The page itself, sampled each animation frame: 0 of 482 samples with anything but one arm. Frames also looked at by eye on a contact sheet. On slow 4G after the edit: 0 of 635.
4. **Throttled mobile trace: pass.** 12 runs of 10 s, Pixel 7 profile, 4x CPU, 6 on slow 4G and 6 unthrottled network. Loop tasks (after 5 s): longest 3.2 ms. Layout shifts: 0. Dropped frames: 0. A load-time task, before the loop starts, sometimes passes 50 ms: 4 of 12 runs, 61 ms at most. The unchanged reference does the same: 6 of 12, 62 ms at most. Recorded with Chrome's trace through Playwright, not the DevTools MCP.
5. **Reduced motion: pass.** Two screenshots 2 s apart are identical, in Chrome and in WebKit.
6. **Motion standards** (`review-animations/STANDARDS.md`): no blocking finding. Rare page, so delight is allowed; cuts need no easing; reduced motion removes the loop.
   - It prefers CSS for predetermined motion; this uses timers. The brief asks for that (timings from `routine.json`), and the cost is 3.2 ms at most.
   - Reduced motion is read once at load; switching it on mid-visit takes effect on reload.
   - Not in the standards: the loop never ends and has no pause button (WCAG 2.2.2).

Also checked: with the tab reported as hidden, 0 swaps in 4 s, and the loop resumes, in both browsers.
